Σ|dq| and flags

Σ|dq| is joint-space path length in radians, not Cartesian TCP travel and not the chord |qend−qstart|. For each of the six joints the cubic spline is sampled at 100 Hz; that joint’s mileage is Σi|q(ti+1)−q(ti)|. Σ|dq| is the sum of those six numbers. A joint that goes out and back counts twice.
